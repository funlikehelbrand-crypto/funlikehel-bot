Frequently asked questions

Can I try SurfIQ for free? +

Yes! We offer 14 days of free access with no credit card required. Just fill out the demo form — we'll set up your account within 24 hours.

How long does onboarding take? +

Basic setup takes 1-2 days. Full onboarding with data migration and team training — usually a week. We help at every step.

Can I import data from another system? +

Yes. We import data from Excel, Google Sheets, SurfCloud, and other systems. Migration is included in the Enterprise plan.

How do AI agents work? +

AI agents automatically reply to customer messages across 6 channels (Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook, email, SMS, TikTok). They know your offer, pricing, and availability. They respond in <2 minutes, 24/7.

Does SurfIQ work on mobile? +

Yes — the panel is fully responsive. Instructors check their schedule on their phone, and you manage your school from any device.

What sports does SurfIQ support? +

Kitesurfing, Windsurfing, Wing Foil, SUP, Surfing, Wakeboarding, Pumpfoil, and more. The system is flexible — you can add your own disciplines and courses.

Can I manage multiple locations? +

Yes! SurfIQ supports multiple locations in one panel. We've tested it across 2 locations (Poland + Egypt) over 2 full seasons.

Plans start at 499 PLN/mo (Starter) up to 1,499 PLN/mo (Enterprise). AI and KiteSafari modules are priced individually. No hidden costs — cancel anytime.
